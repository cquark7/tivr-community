# AI Image Upscaler

Enlarge a photo up to 4x with on-device AI, then check the result with a before and after slider.

**[Open AI Image Upscaler →](https://tivr.dev/image-upscaler)** · [Explore all tivr tools](../../README.md#find-your-next-tool)

## What you can do

- 4x or 2x output, processed in tiles on your device.
- Compare the original and the result side by side.
- Export as JPEG, WebP, or PNG — JPEG by default.
- Uses the GPU when available, with a CPU fallback.
