# Background Remover

Remove an image background and download a transparent PNG.

**[Open Background Remover →](https://tivr.dev/background-remover)** · [Explore all tivr tools](../../README.md#find-your-next-tool)

## What you can do

- Cuts out objects, products, and animals, not just portraits.
- Transparent PNG output, ready to composite.
- Compare the original and the cutout side by side.
- Refine the result with restore and erase tools
